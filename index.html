Common Questions

Answers Before You Ask

Most first-time patients have the same questions before they walk in. Here's what we'd want you to know — and you'll get personalised answers in your free initial consultation.

Still curious? Message us on WhatsApp or book a free consultation.
Are the treatments safe?

Every treatment at ShapeLab is doctor-led — designed and supervised by Dr. Katherine E. Hernandez, a UK-registered medical doctor and Aesthetic Medicine specialist. We use FDA-recognised devices and clinical-grade products only, and every patient receives a full consultation and body composition assessment before anything is administered.

Will it hurt? What's the downtime?

Most of our treatments are non-surgical and minimally invasive. Discomfort is typically mild — closer to a sensation than a pain — and topical numbing is applied where appropriate. Downtime varies by treatment: many have none at all (you can return to work or social plans the same day); a few have 24–72 hours of mild redness or swelling. Your doctor will walk you through exactly what to expect for the treatment you're considering.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

You don't need to know — that's the point of the free initial consultation. Tell us your goals (smoother skin, more contoured body, more energy, more confidence), and Dr. Hernandez will recommend a personalised treatment plan based on your concerns, your body composition, and what's clinically right for you. There's no pressure to commit, and many patients leave with a phased plan they can pace at their own comfort.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on the treatment. Some — like IV drip therapy or signature facials — show effects within 24 hours. Others, like microneedling or body contouring, build gradually over 4–8 weeks as collagen rebuilds and tissues respond. Your treatment plan will include a realistic timeline and check-in milestones so you know what to expect at each stage.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes — and very often we recommend it. Aesthetic results compound when treatments are layered intelligently. For example, body contouring pairs well with skin tightening; microneedling pairs well with topical actives. Your personalised plan will sequence treatments in the right order, with the right gaps between them, to maximise results without overlapping recovery windows.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the treatment and your personalised plan. The initial consultation and body composition analysis are complimentary — there's no obligation to proceed afterwards. During the consultation we'll go through pricing transparently for the treatments that fit your goals, and we offer phased plans for patients who'd rather pace investment over time.
