About ShapeLab

Puerto Princesa's Premier Aesthetic Clinic

Located in the heart of Puerto Princesa City, ShapeLab Aesthetic Clinic is a doctor-led center dedicated to non-surgical body sculpting, skin renewal, and holistic wellness. Every treatment begins with a complimentary body composition analysis and a personalized consultation — because your journey is uniquely yours.

Dr. Katherine E. Hernandez
Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Katherine E. Hernandez

Where science meets artistry — UK-registered medical doctor & aesthetic medicine specialist.

UK-Registered · 2023 Aesthetic Medicine · 2025 PALAA Member

With a foundation rooted in nursing, Dr. Hernandez built a deep understanding of patient care — one that now defines her refined and meticulous approach to aesthetics. After earning her medical degree and passing the Physician Licensure Examination in 2019, she elevated her credentials internationally, becoming a UK-registered doctor in 2023.

Her passion for advanced aesthetic treatments led her to specialize in Aesthetic Medicine in 2025. She is an active member of the Philippine Academy of Aesthetic and Age Management Inc. At ShapeLab, she curates each treatment with intention — blending medical precision with aesthetic vision to sculpt, enhance, and refine natural beauty.
