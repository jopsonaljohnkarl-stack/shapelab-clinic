Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 May 2026 · Effective: 10 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShapeLab Aesthetic Clinic ("ShapeLab," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit shapelab-clinic.vercel.app or interact with our services. We are a doctor-led aesthetic clinic operating in Puerto Princesa City, Palawan, Philippines, and we comply with the Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you actively provide it or when it is automatically captured for essential site operation.

Information you give us directly

  • Booking enquiries — when you click through to our external booking platform, you may be asked to provide your name, contact number, email address, and treatment preferences.
  • Direct messages — if you contact us via WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, phone, or email, we receive whatever information you send.
  • Consultation records — during in-clinic consultations, we collect medical history, lifestyle information, and body composition data necessary to provide safe, personalised treatment.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs — our hosting provider (Vercel) records standard request data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamp. This is used for security and uptime monitoring.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see "Cookies" below.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and confirm appointments.
  • To provide medical consultations and aesthetic treatments tailored to your goals.
  • To maintain accurate clinical records as required by law.
  • To improve the website experience (anonymised analytics only — no personal profiling).
  • To send appointment reminders or post-treatment follow-ups when you have explicitly opted in.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing.

Cookies

This website uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies, all essential or analytical:

  • Session cookies set by our hosting provider (Vercel) for security and load balancing. These expire when you close your browser.
  • Embedded services — our location map (Google Maps) and web fonts (Google Fonts) may set cookies governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control these directly.
  • Local storage — we use your browser's local storage only to remember whether you've dismissed notices like the cookie banner. This data never leaves your device.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not prevent the site from functioning, though embedded maps may not display.

Third-Party Services

We rely on several third parties to operate the website and clinic. Each has its own privacy practices:

  • Vercel Inc. — website hosting and content delivery. Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC — Google Maps embed (location), Google Fonts (typography), Google Search (indexing). Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — only when you click our Facebook page link. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when you click our WhatsApp link to message us, your communication is governed by WhatsApp's terms.
  • External booking platform — bookings are handled by book.easethetics.ph. The information you submit there is governed by that platform's privacy policy.

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Personal information collected through the website is stored on Vercel's secure cloud infrastructure. Clinical records are stored on encrypted, access-controlled systems within the clinic. We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by Philippine law for medical records (typically 15 years for adult patient records, longer for paediatric).

Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

As a data subject under Republic Act No. 10173, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about what data we collect and why.
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Object to processing for marketing purposes.
  • Erasure or blocking of your data when there is no longer a lawful purpose to keep it.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at privacy.gov.ph if you believe your rights have been violated.

Children's Privacy

Our services and website are not directed at minors under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our site, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
